Hi, I'm Harald!

I am a fourth year Ph.D. student in the Data Systems Lab at KTH Royal Institute Technology, advised by Paris Carbone and Seif Haridi. My research focuses on consensus and transactions in distributed systems. More specifically, I aim to make protocols more efficient by leveraging practical aspects such as the application workload and network. I am the creator of the OmniPaxos project. I have previously interned at FIS Global and RISE Research Institutes of Sweden.
